Warm community needs clear boundaries.

EYS aims to create welcoming spaces while taking concerns, vulnerabilities, referrals and event safety seriously.

How EYS keeps participation safer.

Safeguarding is part of the operating model: welcome people well, do not promise specialist help beyond EYS capacity, and escalate serious concerns through appropriate routes.

  • Clear roles Volunteers, mentors and hosts should understand what they can offer, what they cannot offer, and when to refer.
  • Appropriate signposting EYS can help people find support, but regulated advice, crisis support and specialist services must be handled by qualified providers.
  • Event care Gatherings should consider welcome, consent around photography, accessibility, respectful conduct and safe follow-up.
  • Concern reporting Members, families and partners should have a simple way to raise concerns confidentially.

Raise a safeguarding concern.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first. For EYS activity concerns, email the team with as much relevant context as you can safely share.
