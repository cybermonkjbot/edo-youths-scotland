Public benefit with clear accountability.

EYS is being shaped as a community interest platform: culturally grounded, practical, careful with trust, and focused on visible public benefit.

What EYS measures.

Impact is not only attendance. EYS tracks whether people are welcomed, supported, made visible, referred responsibly and connected to lawful pathways for contribution.

  • Participation Members, volunteers, mentors, partners and attendees who take part in community activity.
  • Visibility Approved member profiles, showcases, stories, businesses, talents and achievements made public.
  • Opportunity Referrals, mentoring, training, work readiness, partner introductions and practical next steps.
  • Belonging Community circles, welfare signposting, cultural connection and reduced isolation.

Governance commitments

A complete CIC website needs transparent standards. These are the operating commitments the site is now structured to support.

Public benefit first

Programmes, partnerships and member visibility are assessed through their community benefit and dignity.

Safe member publishing

Joining requests remain private. Public profiles are admin-approved and publish only appropriate details.

Responsible referrals

EYS can signpost support, but regulated advice and specialist support must go through qualified providers.

Governance and team visibility.

Public profiles can be published as directors, advisers and operational leads are confirmed. The admin workflow keeps draft details private until approved.
