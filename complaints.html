Feedback should have a route.

EYS should be easy to praise, question or challenge. Complaints help the organisation learn, protect trust and improve community practice.

What you can raise.

You can raise concerns about events, member conduct, volunteering, communication, privacy, public profile information, partner activity or how EYS handled your request.

  • Tell us what happened Include dates, names, the activity involved, what went wrong and what outcome you are looking for.
  • Confidential handling EYS should limit complaint details to the people who need them to review and respond.
  • Fair review The team should acknowledge, review, respond and record learning where appropriate.
  • Urgent risk If someone is at immediate risk, contact emergency services or the relevant specialist service first.

Make a complaint or send feedback.

Use a clear subject line so the team can route your message. If the issue is sensitive, say so at the top of the email.
