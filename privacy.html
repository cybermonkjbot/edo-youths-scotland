Careful with people. Careful with data.

EYS collects only the information needed to respond to joining requests, support participation, publish approved profiles and manage community activity.

What information EYS handles.

The site collects joining details, messages, member profile information and admin account information. Public member profiles are separate from private joining requests and are published only after admin review.

  • Joining requests Name, email, phone if provided, location, interest, message and optional profile photo metadata are used to follow up with the person who contacted EYS.
  • Member profiles Public profiles show only approved information such as name, role, location, bio, skills, business name, website link or approved image URL.
  • Admin access Admin sessions are used to protect joining requests, member profile controls and blog publishing.
  • Contact by email Emails sent to EYS are handled so the team can answer, support, refer or follow up appropriately.

Privacy principles

EYS should keep private information private, publish only with care, and remove or correct information when someone reasonably asks.

Purpose limitation

Information is used for membership, volunteering, partnership, support, profile approval and community administration.

Minimum public data

Joining request contact details are not shown in the public member directory.

Correction and removal

Members can ask EYS to correct, hide or remove their public profile information.

Questions about your information?

Email EYS if you want your details corrected, your public profile changed, or your joining request removed where appropriate.
