From Heritage To Opportunity

Why EYS exists

EYS exists to help young people earn, learn and belong without losing identity, dignity or cultural pride.

A Scotland-based platform for visibility, practical support and cultural confidence, built around the simple promise that community should open doors.

A platform where belonging becomes contribution.

Edo Youths Scotland was born from a simple truth: people thrive when they are seen, supported and connected.

The Spark

We are Edo by heritage, Scottish by contribution, and global by possibility.

EYS gives young people a trusted place to showcase professions, businesses, talents and achievements while building pathways into skills, enterprise, employment, mentorship and community leadership.

The Gap

Ambition is present. Talent is present. Culture is present. The platform is often missing.

Many minority youths need trusted routes into visibility, lawful work, welfare signposting, enterprise readiness and social connection. EYS turns that need into a structured bridge.

The Bridge

A minority-led CIC agency that turns cultural belonging into lawful opportunity.

The work is proudly Edo-rooted and open to wider public benefit: serving members, families, Scottish organisations, emerging businesses, volunteers and local residents.
