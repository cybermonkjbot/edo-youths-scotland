Events

Gatherings that turn visibility into opportunity.

EYS events bring culture, learning, enterprise and care into the same room so members can meet, contribute and grow with confidence.

Event strands

The programme is designed for real participation: welcoming new faces, showcasing member talent and connecting people to lawful routes for work, enterprise and service.

Culture and belonging nights

Music, food, stories and friendship for members, families and friends of the community.

Member showcases

Professional, business, creative and volunteer spotlights that make hidden contribution visible.

Skills and enterprise sessions

Practical workshops for employability, communication, leadership and trading readiness.

Built for members, partners and the wider public benefit.

EYS events are warm, structured and purposeful. Each gathering should help people feel less isolated, more visible and better connected to responsible opportunity.

  • For young people Meet peers, find mentors, discover opportunities and share your work, talent or ambition.
  • For families Build trusted community relationships and help younger members stay connected to culture and care.
  • For partners Engage with a minority-led platform focused on fair work, inclusion, service and local contribution.
  • For volunteers Support hosting, welcome, event delivery, photography, showcases, mentoring and follow-up.

Host, sponsor or support an EYS gathering.

Useful support includes venues, transport, catering, photography, training, sponsorship and safe referral routes.
